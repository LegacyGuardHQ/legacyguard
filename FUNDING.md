# LegacyGuard Funding and Sponsor Ethics

## Purpose

Funding is intended to convert tested software into a responsibly operated
service. LegacyGuard should not accept sensitive continuity information on an
unsafe free hosting arrangement.

LegacyGuard is alpha software with a public [GitHub Sponsors profile](https://github.com/sponsors/jh505tt-create).
Sponsorship is general support for responsible development; it does not make
the application production-ready or approved for real personal or sensitive
data. Use synthetic data only.

Last checked 2026-10-20: the profile is publicly accessible, but publication
of monthly or one-time tiers has not been verified. Check the profile for
current sponsorship options and their specific benefits before promoting
tiers; this document does not promise tier rewards.

Do not send money or payment details in response to an issue, pull request,
discussion, or unsolicited message. Sponsorships are not represented as
tax-deductible.

The provisional target is $250 per month for an initial six-month period. This
is a partial funding goal for independent security and privacy review,
accessibility and usability work, and safe demonstration prerequisites; it is
not a promise to fully fund an audit or launch. Public updates should report
the funding goal, milestone status and changes, conflicts, and aggregate use
of funds without exposing sponsor or transaction details.

## Priorities

Funds should support dated, verifiable milestones in this order:

1. Independent application and infrastructure security review.
2. Privacy and appropriate legal guidance.
3. Protected pilot hosting, monitoring, backups, and recovery testing.
4. Accessibility and stress-sensitive usability testing.
5. Incident response and responsible disclosure operations.
6. Documentation, community support, and sustainable maintenance.

## Sponsor independence

Sponsorship does not provide access to user records, private research,
credentials, or sensitive systems. It does not purchase security exceptions,
hidden roadmap control, preferential vulnerability treatment, misleading
endorsements, or ownership of community contributions.

LegacyGuard should publish material goals, milestone status, changes in scope,
conflicts of interest, aggregate use of funds, and donated services that could
influence vendor decisions. Sponsor recognition is optional and must not imply
certification or control.

Sponsorship is general support; this document does not offer tier rewards or
promise refunds. Any refund or billing matter is subject to the applicable
GitHub Sponsors terms. Before publishing tiers, publish their prices and
benefits and explain how milestone changes and refund requests will be handled.
Do not accept restricted gifts without clear, public terms. Decline funding
conditions that conflict with the mission, independence, user safety, or
applicable law.
