# LegacyGuard Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LegacyGuard is committed to participation without harassment, humiliation,
coercion, discrimination, or exploitation. The project concerns difficult life
events and sensitive family information; participation must protect dignity as
carefully as it protects code.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people and their experiences with respect.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking the person presenting them.
- Use inclusive, plain language and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Ask permission before sharing another person's story or identifying details.
- Make space for different abilities, cultures, family structures, and levels
  of technical confidence.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Report suspected vulnerabilities privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discrimination or demeaning language based on identity, disability, age,
  family structure, grief, health, finances, or technical ability.
- Publishing private information or sensitive stories without informed consent.
- Manipulating fear, grief, illness, or financial insecurity to sell, recruit,
  or win an argument.
- Pressuring maintainers to skip security, privacy, accessibility, or legal
  review.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Deliberate disruption, impersonation, spam, or undisclosed promotion.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, events, research
sessions, private project communications, and public spaces when someone is
representing LegacyGuard.

A confidential community-reporting channel must be established before public
participation opens. Possible responses include a private correction, content
removal, a warning, participation limits, suspension, or removal. No founder,
sponsor, maintainer, or partner is exempt.

This code does not prohibit respectful criticism, difficult questions, or
evidence-based disagreement. It governs how people are treated while those
disagreements are resolved.
